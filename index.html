Welcome to Yoga

Yoga is more than just physical exercise – it is a path to inner peace, strength, and flexibility of both body and mind. In yoga classes, you will learn how to release stress, improve posture, and boost energy through mindful breathing and controlled movements.

With regular practice, you will develop balance, endurance, and mental clarity while discovering a deeper connection with yourself.

Join us to find harmony and inner strength together!

Founded in Belgrade, Eterna was born from a desire to create a sanctuary in the middle of a busy city.

Our mission is to make yoga accessible to everyone, regardless of age or fitness level. We believe that by changing the way we breathe and move, we can change the way we live our lives off the mat.
